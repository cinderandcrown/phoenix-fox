# Privacy Policy — Phoenix Fox

**Publisher:** Cinder & Crown LLC  
**Effective:** 14 November 2026  
**App:** Phoenix Fox (`com.cinderandcrown.phoenixfox`)

This policy describes how the Phoenix Fox iOS app handles information. It is the source for the in-app Privacy screen. Host the same text at a public HTTPS URL before App Store sale.

## Summary

- Cinder & Crown does **not** run a Phoenix Fox cloud account and does **not** collect analytics, advertising IDs, or crash data from the app by default.
- What you say, type, or capture is processed **on your iPhone** or sent to **services you choose** (your AI provider keys, your own relay, optional self-hosted telemetry).
- Meta’s apps and glasses software handle pairing under Meta’s terms when you connect compatible AI glasses.

## Data on your iPhone

Phoenix Fox stores settings, API keys and tokens (Keychain), optional notes and memories, accessibility data you enable (for example consented face labels), and caches needed to run on-device models. You can erase local app data from **Settings → Privacy & Data**.

## Cloud AI providers (optional)

If you paste an API key or sign in to a provider (for example xAI Grok, OpenAI / ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini) or connect your own assistant relay, then:

- Your voice transcripts, typed prompts, and any photos you attach to a request are sent to that provider so it can answer.
- Those providers process data under **their** privacy policies and terms. Phoenix Fox does not sell that data.
- You must accept in-app **Cloud AI data sharing** consent before the first cloud send. You can withdraw consent in Privacy & Data; the app then stays on on-device answers until you accept again.

## Glasses and Meta

Connecting glasses uses Meta’s Wearables Device Access Toolkit and the Meta AI app. Camera frames are requested only when you ask Jarvis to look (or use a feature that needs a look). The capture light on the glasses stays on while the camera is in use; Phoenix Fox cannot turn that light off. Meta’s handling of pairing and device data is governed by Meta’s policies.

## Permissions

Phoenix Fox requests system permissions only for features you use (microphone, speech recognition, Bluetooth, local network for glasses Wi-Fi streaming, photos, location for place reminders / walking directions, calendars, reminders, contacts, and others listed in the system prompts). You can deny a permission; the related feature will not work.

## Purchases

If subscriptions are offered through the App Store, Apple processes payment. Cinder & Crown receives entitlement status from StoreKit, not your full payment card details.

## Children

Phoenix Fox is not directed at children under 13 and is not enrolled in Apple’s Made for Kids program.

## Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: use **Settings → Privacy & Data → Report a concern**, or email the publisher address listed on the App Store product page.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change; continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy when you next open the app’s Privacy screen or accept cloud AI consent again if the cloud-sharing terms change.
